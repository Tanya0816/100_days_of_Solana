This is day 24 of learning of Solana 

I explored accounts while building account Explorer

Every Solana account stores its state as a flat array of bytes. The account is defined by how the bytes in the account should be interpreted by the program that owns the account.

While working on the explorer, I got fields like 'lamports', 'owner', and 'data'. 

The data part was just a blob of raw bytes. Today, I explored this data part.
