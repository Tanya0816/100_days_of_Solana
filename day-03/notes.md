1. SOL and Lamports: 
   In Solana, SOL is the native token and is used for tasks like transactions, funds, accounts or even for staking purpose.

   A lamport is the smallest unit of SOL . All the work for which SOL is used, they are converted into lamports at runtime.
   Lamports are always a whole numbers.  

   1 lamport = 1/1_000_000_000 SOL

   Some common lamport amounts are :
      => 5000 lamports = 0.000005 SOL
      => 890880 lamports = ~0.00089 SOL
      => 1000000000 lamports = 1 SOL
      => 20000000000 lamports = 20 SOL

Lamports are used instead of SOL in Solana to avoid decimal arithmetic .
