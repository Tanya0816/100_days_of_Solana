 Three ways of using wallet -
1. CLI wallet :
   using commands like "solana-keygen , solana address and solana balance" in terminal , anyone can get their wallet in the terminal . 

2. Browser extension wallet:
  In this, a script is used to install(if not already) and connect the wallet from the browser to our local device. 
  These wallets are safer to use as compared to CLI wallets. 

3. Mobile Wallet:
   Mobile wallets often support biomtric authentication alongside a password. The seed phrase backup flow in this wallet is same as in case of browser wallet. 

Two type of wallets-

1. Hardware wallet:
   In this , the private key is stored in a physical device . The key is never shared even at the time of signing a transaction.
   It is a cold wallet.

2. Multisig wallet:
   Requires multiple people to approve a transaction before execution. 

Each wallet has its pros and cons , one needs to select a wallet based on the requirements and the level of security.
