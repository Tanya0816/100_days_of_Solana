1. Reading on-chain data :
   On-chain data refers to all the information that is recorded directly on the blockchain. Every transaction, every token swap, every wallet interaction is stored permanently and can be accessed publicly. 

   In trading, on-chain data gives an information advantage over other market participants as everything is transparent and is accessible. 

   In Solana, this concept is used to identify market opportunities , track smart money , and so on.

   To retrieve on-chain data in Solana, developers query data from a validator or an indexer using the JSON RPC API.
