Exploring token economics and incentives - 

In Solana SOL is the token which is the fuel that powers every transaction. 

The concept to tokens in Web3 is similar to the concept of credits, reward points in Web2 . 

Today was about connecting the token concept from Web2. As learning proceeds, I'll deep dive into this concept. 

Ran some commands in terminal to build a token (a digital asset) from scratch. 

Here are the commands -

1. solana config set --url devnet
2. solana airdrop 2
3. spl-token create-token
4. spl-token create-account YOUR_TOKEN_MINT_ADDRESS
5. spl-token mint YOUR_TOKEN_MINT_ADDRESS 100
6. spl-token supply YOUR_TOKEN_MINT_ADDRESS
7. spl-token accounts
8. spl-token display YOUR_TOKEN_MINT_ADDRESS
