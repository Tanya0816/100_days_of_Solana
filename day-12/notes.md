Let's understand the importance of devnet and mainnet-

Devnet: 
It is used by developer at the time of development to test their dApp before launching or deploying in mainnet where real money is at play. 
Using this give developers freedom to test their dApp without investing a single penny. 
It also help developer by giving them a place to test the functianlity by giving  almost same environment as in mainnet.  
In devnet, tokens used are not real .

Testnet:
It is specifically designed by validators and core developers
It is used by Solana developers to check and test new release feature before launching on the main chain.

Mainnet:
It is where the real money is used as a meand of exchange or any other function.
Once a dApp is deployed to mainnet, changes become much more sensitive because real users and assets are involved. Some programs are upgradeable, while others may be intentionally made immutable.
If the app is launched wihtout proper testing and is associated with money, it can cause a huge loss by the dapp getting hacked inthe mainnet.

NOTE:
The reason why testnet is used for final testingis because devnet is centralized while testnet is entirely decentralized. Testnet runs on a newer and more experimental software release branch than mainnet and testnet.
